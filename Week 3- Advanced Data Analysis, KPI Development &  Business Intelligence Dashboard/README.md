## Week 3- Advanced Data Analysis, KPI Development & Business Intelligence Dashboard
# Project: Sample Superstore Sales Dashboard (Advanced Analysis)
An interactive Power BI dashboard built for a retail Superstore dataset, giving management a real-time view of sales, profit, and performance across products, regions, and customer segments.

# Key figures:
* Total Sales: $2.3M
* Total Profit: $286.4K
* Total Orders: 5K
* Average Discount: $0.16
* Profit Margin: 12.5%

# Key Findings:
* Customer Segment: Consumer leads in sales and profit, while Home Office has the highest profit margin.
* Monthly Performance: November records the highest sales, while February is the weakest month.
* Loss-Making Products: The Top 5 loss-making products account for the largest individual losses, led by a loss of -$8,879.97.
* Regional Performance: South has the lowest sales but strong efficiency, while Central has the weakest profit margin.
* Yearly Profit Growth: Total profit increased consistently from $50K in 2014 to $93K in 2017, despite slower growth in 2017.
* Discount & Profitability: Furniture has the highest average discount and the lowest profit, indicating that heavy discounting may be hurting its profitability.

# Tools used: Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX
