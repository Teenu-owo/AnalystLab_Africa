## Project: Sample Superstore Sales Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard built for a retail Superstore dataset, giving management a real-time view of sales, profit, and performance across products, regions, and customer segments.

# Key Figures
* Total Sales: $2M
* Total Profit: 286K
* Total Orders: 10K
* Profit Margin: 12.47%
* Average Sales: $229.86

# Key findings:
* West is the company’s strongest-performing region, generating the highest sales and profit compared with the other regions.

* Technology leads category sales, but performance is fairly balanced across the three categories.

* The analysis identified the top five most profitable products, with Canon imageCLASS 2000 Advanced Copier recording the highest profit.

* The Consumer segment contributes the most revenue, generating approximately $1.16M, compared with the Corporate and Home Office segments.

* Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, with a slight decline from January to February, followed by an increase in March. Sales remain relatively stable from April to August, before rising significantly in September. After a brief decline in October, sales reach their highest level in November and remain strong in December.

Dashboard includes: 5 KPI cards, 5 visualizations (bar charts, column charts, area chart, pie chart), and 2 interactive slicers (Region, Category).

# Tools used: Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX
