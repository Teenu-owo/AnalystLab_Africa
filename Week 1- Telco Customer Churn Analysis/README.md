## Week 1: Business Analytics Case Study — Customer Churn Analysis (ABC Communications Ltd, Telco Customer Churn dataset)
A few key findings:
* Overall customer churn rate was 26.5%.
* New customers recorded the highest churn rate at 47.4%.
* Month-to-month contract customers had the highest churn.
* Electronic check users recorded approximately 45% churn.
* Fibre optic customers showed higher churn compared to other internet services.
